About the Project

This website presents a small IT help desk idea for people who need clear computer support.

Purpose

The purpose of the site is to explain basic support services, give useful safety tips, and offer a simple way for visitors to contact the help desk.

Target Users

  • Students who need help with school devices.
  • Parents who want safer home technology.
  • Small users who need basic troubleshooting.
